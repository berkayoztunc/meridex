Security & transparency

Know exactly how your keys are handled.

Meridex is a hosted wallet. Private keys are encrypted at rest in a Cloudflare D1 database with a server-side master key, and only decrypted to sign your transactions. You can export your key at any time with /export.

Meridex currently runs on the Stellar testnet. Do not treat testnet balances as real funds, and only keep on a hosted wallet what you are comfortable risking.

  • Keys encrypted at rest
  • Private chats only — groups are ignored
  • Webhook protected by secret token
  • Runs on Cloudflare Workers
  • Export your key anytime

FAQ

Quick answers.

Can't find yours? Ask the bot with /help.

Is Meridex non-custodial?

No. Keys are generated and stored encrypted by the bot so it can sign for you. You can export your private key and move to any other wallet whenever you like.

Which network does it use?

Stellar testnet today. Mainnet support is built in and will be enabled when ready.

Which pools and providers are supported?

Aquarius AMM pools for swaps and the Stelfun launchpad for bonding-curve coins.

What can I trade?

XLM and tokens that have an Aquarius pool, plus coins from the Stelfun launchpad.

Does it cost anything?

Using the bot is free. Normal Stellar network fees apply to transactions.

Can I have several wallets?

Yes. Create, list and switch wallets with /wallets.

What is slippage?

The price movement you accept before a swap fails. Change it any time with /slippage.

How do I leave with my funds?

Run /export to get your private key and import it into any Stellar wallet.
